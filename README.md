HR Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

Project Overview

This HR Analytics project focuses on analyzing employee data to identify workforce trends, understand employee attrition, and evaluate key HR performance indicators. An interactive Power BI dashboard was developed to help HR teams make informed, data-driven decisions.

Business Objectives

Analyze overall employee headcount and workforce distribution.

Identify employee attrition patterns and trends.

Analyze employee demographics, including age, gender, and department.

Evaluate job satisfaction and employee performance indicators.

Understand salary distribution across different job roles and departments.

Identify factors contributing to employee turnover.
